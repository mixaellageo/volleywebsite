Volleyball, a guide

Volleyball is a popular team sport played by two teams separated by a net. The main goal is to send the ball over the net and make it touch the ground on the opponent’s side. It's an exciting sport for people of all ages


The rules of the game:

  • 6 players per team
  • The ball must pass over the net and land inside the opponent’s court to score.
  • Maximum 3 touches per team
  • Sets are played to 25 points
  • Players cannot hit the ball twice in a row
  • Players cannot catch, hold, or throw the ball
  • Players can use any part of their body to hit the ball
  • The ball is allowed to touch the lines of the court


What do you need to play volleyball:

To play volleyball, you need a ball, a net, and a court. You should wear comfortable sports clothes, trainers, and knee pads to protect yourself. Volleyball is usually played with two teams of six players. You also need teamwork, communication, and skills like serving, passing, and spiking. Most importantly, you need to practise and have fun!
